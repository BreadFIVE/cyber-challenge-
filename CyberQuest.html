Welcome, operative.

A 90-minute capture-the-flag challenge covering cryptography, OSINT, pen testing, forensics, web security and social engineering.

  • Each challenge hides a flag in the format CTF{something_like_this}. Find it, submit it, bank the points.
  • Hints are available but cost points (10% for the first, 30% for the second).
  • You have one clock for everything. Skip hard ones and come back – harder challenges are worth more.
  • The Toolkit tab has crypto helpers and the Pentest Lab tab is a safe, simulated hacking sandbox. Web search is fair game for OSINT, tools and learning.
  • Work in teams of 2–4. Talk to each other. That's how real security teams work.
⚖️ Rules of engagement: Everything here is fictional or simulated. In the real world, only test systems you own or have written permission to test. Unauthorised access is a crime (in the UK, the Computer Misuse Act 1990). Skills for defence, not harm.

Progress saves in this browser, so refreshing won't lose your score. Use one device per team.

Double-check spellings.

Flag tips

  • Flags look like CTF{...}. Not case-sensitive, but spelling and underscores matter.
  • OSINT answers (like a city) are lowercase: CTF{paris}.
  • Stuck? Try the simplest thing first. Most challenges are puzzles, not brute force.
